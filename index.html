Value Chain Activity Purposes (Layer 1)

Plan
Ensures a shared understanding of the vision, current status, and improvement direction for all four dimensions and all products and services across the organization.
Engage
Provides a good understanding of stakeholder needs, transparency, continual engagement, and good relationships with all stakeholders.
Design & Transition
Ensures that products and services continually meet stakeholder expectations for quality, costs, and time to market. The quality gate before go-live.
Obtain / Build
Ensures that service components are available when and where they are needed, and meet agreed specifications. Think: do we have all the parts? Servers, software, configs — everything needed to assemble the service.
Deliver & Support
Ensures that services are delivered and supported according to agreed specs. Think: is it running and are users getting help? Day-to-day operations, incidents, service requests — after the components are live.
Improve
Ensures continual improvement of products, services, and practices across all value chain activities and the four dimensions of service management.

Key Exam Traps

Obtain/Build vs Deliver & Support
Obtain/Build = components ready (parts, specs, availability)
Deliver & Support = service running, users helped (operations, incidents, requests)
Key word: "components" = Obtain/Build. "Services" = Deliver & Support.
SLM: Design & Transition vs Plan
Plan = performance data & trends (numbers)
Design & Transition = customer feedback (what customers said)
SRM: Design & Transition vs Deliver & Support
Design & Transition = initiates standard changes
Deliver & Support = keeps users productive
Service Desk vs Incident Management
Service desk = captures demand (single point of contact)
Incident management = resolves incidents (restore service ASAP)
Incident vs Problem Management
Incident = restore service ASAP (fix the symptom)
Problem = find the root cause (fix the cause)
Standard vs Normal vs Emergency Change
Standard = pre-approved, low risk, routine (SRM initiates these)
Normal = assessed, authorized, scheduled by change authority
Emergency = urgent, expedited authorization
SLM Watermelon Effect
SLA metrics show green but users complain — data not based on business outcomes. Green outside, red inside.
Utility vs Warranty
Utility = what a service does (fit for purpose)
Warranty = how a service performs (fit for use)
Known Error vs Workaround
Known error = a problem that has been analyzed but not resolved
Workaround = a temporary fix to reduce impact while the root cause remains
Continual Improvement Methods
Use a few key methods suited to the types of improvement the organization handles. Not one method for everything, not as many as possible, not a new one each time.
